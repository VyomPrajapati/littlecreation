Shipping policy
At LITTLE CREATION, we are committed to delivering your baby products safely, quickly, and reliably across the United Kingdom.

Order Processing Time
All orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays).

Once your order has been placed, you will receive an order confirmation email. When your order has been dispatched, a shipping confirmation email with tracking details will be sent to you.

Please note that personalised or engraved items may require additional processing time of 2–5 business days.

Shipping Rates & Delivery Times
Standard UK Shipping
Delivery Time: 2–4 business days
Cost: £3.99
Free UK Shipping

Express UK Shipping
Delivery Time: 1–2 business days

Delivery times are estimates and may vary during busy periods, promotional sales, or postal service delays.

Shipping Partners
We work with trusted UK courier services such as Royal Mail, Evri, and other reliable delivery partners to ensure your order arrives safely.

Tracking Your Order
Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email so you can monitor your parcel’s journey.

If you do not receive tracking information within 3 business days, please contact our support team.

Delays & Delivery Issues
While we do our best to ensure timely delivery, occasional delays may occur due to factors beyond our control, including:

courier delays
severe weather conditions
public holidays
high seasonal demand
We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Incorrect Address Information
Please ensure that all shipping details are entered correctly at checkout.

We cannot be held responsible for orders delayed or lost due to incorrect or incomplete address information provided by the customer.

If you notice an error in your shipping address, please contact us as soon as possible after placing your order.

Lost or Damaged Parcels
If your parcel arrives damaged or appears to be lost in transit, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery or expected delivery date.

We will work quickly with the courier to resolve the issue and arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate.
